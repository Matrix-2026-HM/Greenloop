How Circular Businesses Operate

  1. Design for longevity

    Create products that last longer and are repair-friendly.

  2. Resource recovery

    Collect waste and convert it into raw materials for new products.

  3. Sharing platforms

    Use models that maximize utilization (rental, sharing, leasing).

  4. Product as service

    Businesses retain ownership and provide services, ensuring upkeep and reuse.

Example Business Models

Upcycling studio

Turn discarded textiles into fashion accessories.

Refurbish & lease

Refurbish electronics and lease them to customers.

Material marketplace

Connect waste generators with manufacturers needing recycled feedstock.

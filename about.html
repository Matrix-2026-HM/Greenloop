About Circular Economy & Green Entrepreneurship

The circular economy is an economic system aimed at eliminating waste and the continual use of resources. Green entrepreneurship focuses on creating ventures that are profitable and ecologically sustainable.

Why it matters

Traditional linear models (take-make-waste) are unsustainable. Circular models preserve value and reduce environmental impact while creating economic opportunities.

Key principles

  • Design out waste
  • Keep products and materials in use
  • Regenerate natural systems

Benefits

Job creation, reduced raw material dependency, lower emissions, and resilient local economies.
